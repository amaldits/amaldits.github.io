about Me

I'm Tyan, an Electrical Engineering graduate from Institut Teknologi Bandung with a deep interest in IoT and robotics. My bachelor's thesis focused on autonomous drones for bridge inspection, which sparked my passion for autonomous systems.

I've spent over four years developing firmware for embedded systems, working on projects ranging from agricultural IoT devices to autonomous vehicles. I specialize in sensor integration, real-time systems, and hardware-software interfaces.

What drives me is solving real-world problems through technology. Whether it's optimizing warehouse automation or tracking systems for agriculture, I enjoy taking complex challenges and turning them into practical solutions.

I'm always looking for opportunities to work on innovative projects that combine hardware, software, and intelligent systems.
